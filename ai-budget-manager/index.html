Everything about your money, in one place

Most people use one app for expenses, another for udhar, a third for splitting bills and a spreadsheet for budgets. Money does all of it, and the numbers talk to each other.

Expenses tracked from bank & UPI SMS

Turn on detection and payments from your bank, card and UPI messages appear for review. You approve each one, and the app learns your accounts and categories.

  • Finds the account from the card or account number
  • Recognises self-transfers, card bill payments and refunds
  • Flags duplicates and links messages to entries you typed

Split bills with friends and groups

Paid for dinner, a cab or a trip? Mark it Split with a group or with friends. Only your share counts as your expense, and everyone's share is tracked until it is settled.

  • Trips, flatmates and events with settle-up suggestions
  • Event reports you can share

Udhar khata that adds up

Baki Khata keeps a running balance with every person: what they owe you and what you owe them, including what they owe you in groups.

  • Tag any payment as udhar and pick the person from contacts
  • Corrections keep the history; nothing is silently deleted

Budgets, insights and forecasts

See next month's likely spend learned from your own history, what changed this month, and money that went to or came from other people.

  • Budgets suggested from what you really spend
  • Savings goals with target dates

Credit cards and EMIs under control

Each card shows its outstanding amount, available limit, bill date and amount still to pay. Reminders repeat until the bill is paid, and EMI conversions show the interest and GST.

Accounts

Bank, cash, UPI wallets, cards, loans and investments, grouped like you think about them.

Recurring

Rent, SIPs, subscriptions and EMIs are spotted automatically, with reminders the day before.

Import from Excel

Bring your history from another app as an .xlsx file, or only compare it with Money.

Private by design

A finance app should not need your data. Money has no server and no sign-up.

Stays on your phone

Transactions, khata, groups and messages are stored in the app's private storage and never uploaded.

SMS read on the device

Only when you turn it on, only bank and business senders, and every item waits for your approval.

Honest about ads

The free version loads non-personalised ads when online. Premium has no ads at all. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Is Money free?

Yes. Every feature is free with a few small ads. Premium (₹365 a year through Google Play) removes all ads.

Does Money upload my SMS or bank data?

No. Messages are read and parsed on your phone only when you turn detection on. Your records are stored on the phone and never uploaded. Only a group you choose to share with friends is synced, and only with the people in it. There is no account or sign-up.

Can I keep an udhar khata?

Yes. Baki Khata records money you lent or borrowed per person, with running balances, and any expense can be tagged as udhar in one tap.

How does bill splitting work?

Tag an expense as Split with a group or with friends. Only your share counts as your expense; the rest shows as money others owe you.

Can I move my data from another money app?

Yes. Export your old app's data to Excel (.xlsx) and import it, or only compare it with what you have in Money.

How do I back up my data?

Settings → Privacy & security → Back up now creates a file you keep wherever you like. Restore it on a new phone.
