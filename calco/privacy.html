Calco privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 (first published 31 October 2026)

Calco (“the app”, package com.makcell.calcopia) is a calculator, unit and currency converter, and set of everyday tools. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

Calco doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information. It has no accounts and no analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Your calculation history, your notes and the values you enter into tools
  • Items you delete from history or notes, kept in a Bin on your device for 30 days so you can restore them (you can empty the Bin at any time)
  • Favorite tools and app settings (theme, color, language, number format)
  • If you use Back up, the app writes your history, notes and favorite tools into a file and hands it to Android's share sheet; you choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, Files, or a chat with yourself). Restore reads only the file you pick.
  • The last downloaded currency exchange rates and gold, silver and platinum prices, so they still show offline

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (for example, sharing a note). You can delete your history and notes at any time in the app; deleted items wait in the Bin for 30 days unless you empty it sooner, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Internet use

The app makes these network requests, and only when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Public currency exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com), for the currency converter and the gold, silver and platinum rates (to show prices in rupees).
  • Public gold, silver and platinum prices from gold-api.com, for the Gold & Silver Rates tool. If that service doesn't answer, the app asks a backup source, goldprice.corgano.com, instead.
  • Update checks through Google Play, when you open the app or tap "Check for updates" in Settings.
  • Google Play's in-app rating card, which Google Play may show after you have used the app for a while. Your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.

These requests contain no personal information. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to the service, which handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Permissions

Calco only uses the Internet permission, for the requests described above. It doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or accounts, and it opens no files except a backup file you pick to restore.

Health and date tools

Values you enter in health tools (such as BMI, body fat, ovulation or due-date calculators) are processed on your device and are not sent anywhere. Results are estimates for general information and are not medical advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
